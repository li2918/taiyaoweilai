01 — 前提The Premise

真正的瓶颈很少在技术，
而在 流程本身。 The bottleneck is rarely
the technology. It's the process.

  1. 01

    人工重复工作太多 Too much repetitive labour

    员工大量时间消耗在复制粘贴、整理表格、回复重复问题上。这些工作不是"应该用 AI"——而是本来就不该由人来做。

    Your team burns hours on copy-paste, spreadsheet wrangling, and re-answering FAQs. The question isn't "should we use AI here" — it's why was a human ever doing this.

    Repetitive Toil
  2. 02

    客户跟进不及时 Conversion windows missed

    线索分散在微信、表单、邮件里，跟进全靠记忆。转化窗口在 24 小时内，而你的销售用 48 小时才回复。

    Leads scatter across WeChat, web forms, and inboxes. Sales rely on memory. The conversion window closes in 24 hours — your team replies in 48.

    Lost Conversion
  3. 03

    数据分散，无法决策 Data scattered, decisions delayed

    客户、销售、运营数据散落在 6 个工具里。老板想要一个真实的全局视图——拿不出来。

    Customer, sales, and operations data live across six different tools. Leadership wants a single, truthful dashboard — and can't get one.

    Decision Latency
  4. 04

    运营依赖个人经验 Operations live in people's heads

    核心流程跑在某几个老员工脑子里。新人难以上手，骨干一离职业务就停摆——这不是人的问题，是流程的问题。

    Critical processes live in the heads of a few veterans. New hires can't ramp. When a key person leaves, the business stalls — that's a process problem, not a people problem.

    Tribal Knowledge

"我们提供的不是 AI 工具，而是一整套能让 AI 在你公司真正跑起来的运营升级方案——从诊断、设计到落地、优化。"

"We don't sell you AI tools. We deliver an operational upgrade that makes AI actually work inside your company — from diagnosis and design through deployment and optimization."

02 — AIOP Framework

从流程到自治运营，
五个阶段的完整路径。 A five-stage path from
process to autonomous ops.

每一阶段都交付可见的产出物，不是"咨询完就结束了"。

Every stage ships a concrete artifact. The report is never the deliverable.

  1. 01
    Flow Mapping

    流程拆解 Visualize how the work actually moves.

    把口头描述的"我们怎么干"转成端到端、可视化的流程图。每个环节都标明输入、输出、责任人、耗时。

    Translate "this is how we do it" into an end-to-end process diagram. Every step labelled with inputs, outputs, owners, and elapsed time.

    • End-to-end process diagram
    • RACI & latency matrix
  2. 02
    Bottleneck Analysis

    瓶颈分析 Find what's slow. Find what's broken.

    定位耗时最长、错误最多、最依赖人脑的关键环节。用数据证明，不靠直觉判断。

    Identify the slowest, error-prone, most-human-dependent steps. Backed by data, not gut feel.

    • Critical-path bottleneck report
    • Cost-of-delay quantification
  3. 03
    AI Opportunity Mapping

    AI 机会点识别 Score every gap by value × feasibility.

    用「价值 × 可行性」矩阵评估每个瓶颈的 AI 化空间，圈出优先级最高的 2–3 个突破口。

    Plot every bottleneck on a value-vs-feasibility matrix. Pick the 2–3 highest-leverage interventions to pursue first.

    • Opportunity prioritization matrix
    • ROI projection per intervention
  4. 04
    System Redesign

    流程重构 Rebuild the process around AI & humans.

    重新设计 AI 加入后的流程图，明确人机分工、衡量指标、回滚方案。

    Design the to-be process. Specify human-in-the-loop boundaries, success metrics, and rollback plans.

    • To-be process blueprint
    • Human-in-the-loop design
  5. 05
    Implementation

    落地实施 Ship to production. Stay until it works.

    选型、搭建、培训、上线、监控。我们陪跑直到指标真正达成——不是 demo 通过验收就走人。

    Selection, build, training, launch, monitoring. We stay until the KPI is hit — not just until the demo is signed off.

    • Production rollout & training
    • 30 / 60 / 90-day KPI tracking

06 — 主张Manifesto

客户为何选择我们。 Why companies
choose us.

i.
我们不为了用 AI 而用 AI。
We don't deploy AI for the sake of AI.

先把流程问题搞清楚，再决定哪里需要 AI、哪里只需要规范化。
很多场景，一张清晰的 SOP 比一个 LLM 管用得多。

We map the process first. Only then do we decide what needs AI and what just needs cleaner SOPs.
More often than you'd think, a one-page playbook beats a language model.

ii.
报告不能让公司运转。系统才能。
Reports don't run companies. Systems do.

我们不只是写报告。从方案设计到上线运行，全程陪跑——直到系统真正在你公司每天被使用、被信任、产生回报。

We don't write reports and walk away. We stay with the build, the rollout, the tuning — until the system is used daily, trusted, and earning.

iii.
无法衡量的，我们就不提案。
If we can't measure it, we don't pitch it.

每个方案都绑定可量化指标——节省的人时、提升的转化、缩短的周期、回收的成本。
没有 KPI 的提案，不是提案，是猜测。

Every proposal carries measurable KPIs — hours saved, conversion gained, cycles shortened, cost recovered.
A proposal without numbers isn't a proposal. It's a guess.

iv.
最好的系统，是你能自己运转的系统。
The best system is the one you can keep running.

我们交付的是一套可继承的方法和系统——而不是只有我们能维护的"黑盒"。
培训、文档、运维手册一并交接，让你独立把它跑下去。

What we hand over is a method and a system that you own — not a black box only we can maintain.
Training, documentation, and runbooks come with the build. You keep operating it.
